Who we are

From the Medina to the street

We started with one simple idea: clothes that say something true. No borrowed logos, no empty hype — just heavy cotton, honest prints, and the places that made us.

Our first drop is a tribute to Sfax. The chest carries “صفاقس” in hand-drawn calligraphy; the back carries Bab Diwan, the gate that has watched over the Medina for centuries, framed by olive branches — the tree that feeds our region.
